Choices on species, individual, rating, event and custom become Serval's own filter expression. Choosing deployments or a date range writes the chosen rows to a subset table in the output folder and runs serval extract <subset> -f path -v ALL_VALUES; no Serval change is needed. Counts, size and free space are computed in the background as the selection changes.
